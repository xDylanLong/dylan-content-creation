# Dylan Speaking Series

> A Chinese short-video knowledge base for product judgment, product commercialization, and AI product commercialization.

[中文 README](README.md)

## What this repository is

This repository is for product managers, developers, designers, operators, founders, and other people working with internet products.

The material is organized as progressive learning series. Each series starts with basic questions and moves toward methods, counterexamples, collaboration, execution, and review. A typical episode includes research notes, title and hook options, a complete speaking script, recording guidance, a summary, the next-episode preview, and evidence boundaries.

## Current series

### One-Minute Internet Product Judgment

This series starts with the question “Is this request worth building?” It teaches how to identify users, problems, workaround behavior, real costs, priority, and development risk, then carry the judgment into design, engineering, launch, and review.

Learning path: entry points → core concepts → judgment methods → counterexample boundaries → collaboration and execution → review and integration.

Start here: [1分钟互联网产品判断/00-系列计划.md](1分钟互联网产品判断/00-系列计划.md)

This series asks why someone would pay for a product after it has been built. It covers target users, problem validation, minimum products, cold start, pricing, pilots, value proof, renewal, and the commercialization loop.

## Typical episode structure

Each episode is designed to support both learning and recording:

1. Define the question the episode answers
2. Explain the research basis and evidence strength
3. Provide title, cover direction, and opening hook options
4. Include a complete script ready for recording
5. Add recording notes, visual material, and a closing line
6. Record evidence boundaries, missing material, and audience feedback

## Research and evidence boundaries

Research is used to choose an angle, formulate questions, and check the recent context. It is not proof of audience size, platform popularity, or an industry-wide rule.

The repository distinguishes between:

- Confirmed facts with traceable sources
- Recent signals from public platforms
- Course-derived ideas, hypothetical examples, and judgments that still need validation

Chinese-platform coverage may be incomplete, and some public pages may be unavailable. When that happens, the content keeps the date, source, and coverage limits visible instead of presenting sparse signals as a universal trend.

## How to use this repository

### Learn from the series

Start with `00-系列计划.md` in the relevant directory, then read the episodes in numerical order. The “next episode” and “user feedback / speaking review” sections at the end of each episode can guide continued study and recording retrospectives.

### Record a speaking episode

Use the “口播稿” section of an episode as the recording draft. Markers such as `[停顿]` and `[重读]` are pacing notes and should not be spoken. Re-check hypothetical examples and research boundaries against your own real context before publishing.

### Update the content

When an episode involves a new product, platform, policy, recent case, pricing change, or “latest” context, refresh the research and record the date and sources. Low-volatility concept lessons may reuse earlier research when the reason for reuse and remaining facts to verify are documented.
